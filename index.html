The problem

Your expertise shouldn't be limited by your hours.

Traditional freelancing ties much of your earning capacity to the time you personally spend completing work.

General-purpose AI can accelerate execution, but getting consistent results that reflect a professional's unique methods, judgment, and standards can require significant guidance.

And when projects grow, working alone can become a bottleneck.

We believe there's another way.

A future where professionals can turn their working methods into reusable capabilities, delegate repeatable tasks, and collaborate without losing what makes their work distinctive, on infrastructure anyone can inspect, extend, and improve.

Our vision

Human expertise. AI execution.

DELANCE.AI is exploring an open platform that puts professionals at the centre of AI-powered work.

01

Package your expertise

Turn your methods, workflows, examples, tools, and quality standards into reusable AI capabilities that others can read, fork, and build on.

02

Delegate execution

Use AI agents to carry out defined tasks under your direction, freeing you to focus on judgment, creativity, and decisions that need human expertise.

03

Collaborate without boundaries

Bring together designers, developers, writers, and other specialists. Combine complementary capabilities to work on larger projects as a team.

04

Build on what you create

Explore ways to license capabilities, manage permissions, protect intellectual property, and agree on how revenue is shared.

// These are planned directions for DELANCE.AI, not currently available product features.

For creators

Your way of working is part of your value.

You bring more than a skill. You bring a way of thinking, creating, solving problems, and deciding what good work looks like.

DELANCE.AI aims to help professionals translate that expertise into reusable AI-powered workflows.

  • Develop capabilities that reflect your methods and standards.
  • Delegate repetitive execution while retaining creative and professional oversight.
  • Collaborate with specialists whose capabilities complement yours.
  • Explore new ways to monetize expertise and reusable capabilities.

Create your own way of working. Scale it on your terms.

For businesses

Find the expertise. Combine the capabilities.

Businesses need more than output. They need work that meets their requirements, follows their standards, and solves real problems.

DELANCE.AI envisions a system where businesses can work with specialist creators and combine their expertise with AI-powered execution.

  • Access specialised approaches to defined tasks.
  • Combine complementary capabilities across a project.
  • Establish clear deliverables and quality expectations.
  • Work with human experts who remain accountable for their contributions.

More coordinated expertise. A different model for getting work done.

Open-source platform

Built in the open. Owned by its community.

DELANCE.AI is being designed as an open-source platform, so the infrastructure behind AI-powered freelancing can be inspected, extended, and shaped by the people who use it.

// transparent

Inspectable core

The platform's code, capability formats, and agent workflows are meant to be readable, so creators and businesses can verify how their work is handled.

// extensible

Community capabilities

An open format for packaging methods and tools lets developers and specialists publish, remix, and improve reusable capabilities together.

// self-directed

Contributor-led roadmap

Direction is shaped in public. Developers, designers, and creators can propose features, review decisions, and help set priorities.

# planned workflow, not yet available
$ git clone https://github.com/delance-ai/delance
$ delance capability init --name "brand-voice-editor"
$ delance capability publish --license MIT
# contributors fork, remix, and credit the original author

Open-source licensing, repository structure, and contribution guidelines are planned and will be published with the first public release.

Collaboration

Independent experts. One coordinated team.

A designer's visual language + A developer's methodology + A writer's editorial approach = Something none would make alone

A designer has a distinctive visual language. A developer has an engineering methodology. A writer has an editorial approach.

Together, they can deliver something none of them would produce alone.

DELANCE.AI aims to make these collaborations easier by allowing professionals to contribute specialised capabilities while defining their own participation, permissions, and commercial terms.

The vision is a collaborative workforce in which people and AI agents work together, and independent creators can participate in larger projects without giving up control of their own contributions.

Ownership and trust

Collaboration shouldn't mean giving everything away.

Your expertise is an asset. How it is shared matters. We're exploring a model built around:

01

Creator control

Clear permissions governing how capabilities can be used.

02

Intellectual property

Defined ownership and licensing terms for contributions, including open-source licenses where appropriate.

03

Transparent collaboration

Agreed responsibilities and revenue-sharing arrangements.

04

Human oversight

Professionals retain appropriate control over consequential work and quality.

These principles will need to be supported by clear policies and technical safeguards as the platform develops.
